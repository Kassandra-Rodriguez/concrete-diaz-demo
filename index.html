How we pour

The part you don't see is why it lasts

A cheap pour skips the ground work and cracks in a year. Here is what goes under a Concrete Diaz slab, and why the free estimate is worth the call.

Form and grade

We set the forms to a real slope so water runs off the slab, not toward your house.

Compact the base

Road base is laid and tamped so the slab sits on something solid, not loose dirt.

Steel in every slab

Rebar or mesh runs through the whole pour so it holds together when the ground moves.

Pour, screed and finish

Broom, trowel, stamp or color, done in one visit with the right cure time.

Seal and clean up

Decorative work gets sealed, control joints get cut, and the site leaves clean.
