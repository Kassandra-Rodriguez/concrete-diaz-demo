## The hook

Newer company (2024) but BBB Accredited, competing against 20-year outfits on financing.
No lead form in the ad funnel (ads → Messenger), and the site they do have hides the
"ask for a quote" action. Jobs run roughly $3k–$15k, so a captured lead is worth real
money and financing is a genuine selling point.
